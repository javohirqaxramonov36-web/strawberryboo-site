## 1. State the task

Include your level, format, and constraint. A good prompt defines a checkable output; it does not guarantee truth.

## 2. Separate claims

Mark definitions, checkable facts, and recommendations. Dates, numbers, rules, and requirements should not be accepted without a source.

## 3. Return to a primary source

Use the university’s page for admission requirements and the relevant authority for rules. Check date, scope, and exceptions. A search result headline is not evidence.

## 4. Test with your own example

Replace a generic example with your task. Do not submit generated prose as your lived experience. Automation in the [AI agents course](/en/kurslar/ai-agentlar/) does not remove the need to check inputs and output.

## 5. Leave a note

Record what helped, what you verified, and what remains open. [Prompt Engineering](/en/kurslar/prompt-engineering/) helps you state a clear task, but responsibility for the decision remains yours.
