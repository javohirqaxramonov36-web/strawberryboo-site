## Research/access notes

- Strongest evidence came from official institution homepages/contact/about pages.
- Verification/loading walls blocked direct reads on official domains for Urgench State University, Karshi State Technical University, Karakalpak State University, and NSPI/NSUMT subpages at times; authoritative ministry/university-directory/search evidence was used only as a fallback and is labeled above.
- Fergana State University’s English landing page was inconsistent, but the official FDU sitemap/home/footer was readable and supplied identity/address/contact evidence.
- FSTU and Kokand University are official-domain leads with contact/academic details surfaced in official indexed pages, but their main pages were slow/incomplete during extraction.
- No claims of willingness, capacity, or partnership interest are made. Before outreach, recheck current name, rectorate/international office, and contact details on each site, especially the provisional/blocked entries.
