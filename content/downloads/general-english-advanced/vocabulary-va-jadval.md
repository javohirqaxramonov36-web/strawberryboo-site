# General English C1 — vocabulary va kunlik jadval

## Vocabulary list
- **daily** — kundalik — “I practise English daily.”
- **improve** — yaxshilamoq — “I want to improve my speaking.”
- **example** — misol — “Give me one clear example.”
- **reason** — sabab — “Give one reason for your answer.”
- **review** — qayta ko‘rib chiqmoq — “Review the words on Friday.”

## 7 kunlik jadval
1. 10 so‘z tanlang, har biriga gap yozing.
2. Kechagi so‘zlarni ovoz chiqarib ayting.
3. 5 ta savol tuzing va javob bering.
4. Qisqa audio tinglab, 5 ta kalit so‘zni yozing.
5. So‘zlarni synonym/collocation bilan bog‘lang.
6. 100–150 so‘zlik kichik matn yozing.
7. O‘zingizni tekshiring: nimani ishlata oldingiz, nimani qaytaramiz?
